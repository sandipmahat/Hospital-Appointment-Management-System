Our mission

Built to cut the wait, not the care

Many small and mid-sized hospitals in Nepal still coordinate outpatient scheduling through paper diaries and phone calls, leaving patients unsure whether a slot is even free. sandyHUb replaces that with a single platform where a patient can book, view, edit, or cancel an outpatient appointment in minutes — no queueing at reception, no back-and-forth calls.

Responsive design matters. A Viewport meta tag and a Mobile-first approach keep forms, cards, and appointment tables usable on phones, tablets, and desktop screens.

For patients

Sign in, browse doctors by department, and book one of seven fixed daily time slots — no double-bookings, ever. An account is created automatically the first time you sign in, no separate registration step.

For doctors

Each doctor gets a read-only view of their own schedule — every upcoming appointment, patient, and time slot, without needing to manage bookings themselves.

For administrators

One administrator account reviews every booking, approves or cancels appointments, and provisions doctor logins — with full visibility over every user on the platform.
