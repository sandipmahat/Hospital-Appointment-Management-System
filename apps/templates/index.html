Why Sandycare.me and Healthcare scheduling is an ideal fit for your business

  • HIPAA

    Keep your patient data safe and your business HIPAA compliant. The HIPAA feature restricts access to your system to you and the people to whom you allocate privileges. Client login security and auto logout features ensure that the system is secure from both an admin and patient perspective.

  • Google/Outlook sync calendar

    Don’t let the system book available time when your personal schedule doesn’t allow it. Synchronize your personal and professional calendars to ensure that you don’t book an appointment with a patient when you won’t be able to see them.

  • SOAP

    Maintain appropriate client notes with the SOAP feature, which is available with additional encryption.

  • Import Clients

    If you already have a client list, there is no need to add them to the system manually; you can automate the upload of your client information from an Excel file, cutting the setup time for your online booking system.

  • Intake Forms

    Ensure that you have appropriate information before your patients confirm an appointment with you. You can make these intake forms mandatory, and your patients will not be able to confirm their booking without them.
